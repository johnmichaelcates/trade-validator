Greeks quick reference
Δ Delta
Directional exposure per $1 stock move
0.40–0.60 ATM = balanced risk/reward. 0.10–0.30 OTM = lottery. Also = % chance ITM at expiry.
Θ Theta
Daily time decay cost
Accelerates near expiry. 40–45 DTE is the sweet spot for swing trades.
V Vega
Sensitivity to IV changes
High vega + IV crush after earnings = losses even when stock moves right direction.

Expected move formula
Formula: ATM Call Price + ATM Put Price = Expected Move

Example: Stock at $100. ATM call $4.00 + ATM put $3.50 = $7.50 expected move.
Implied range: $92.50 to $107.50

Rule: If your strike is well outside the expected move, you're betting against the market's consensus. Know it before you click buy.
